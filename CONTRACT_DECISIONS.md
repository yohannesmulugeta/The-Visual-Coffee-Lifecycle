# Contract decisions needed before payable billing

Source: the 14-page scanned Amharic Coffee Processing and Warehousing Contract #001/2018. This is a discussion log; every item below is **unconfirmed**. Record the final Amharic interpretation, authorized approver, effective date, and example calculation before changing billing rules.

| Topic | Current demo behavior | Decision required |
|---|---|---|
| Article 13.1.13, p. 6 | Shows the printed 3,000 / 3,200 ETB rates with an unclear service label; the estimate does not offer this grade. | Confirm the label and whether this service is active. |
| Unwashed grades 4 and 5, p. 6 | Preserves the printed 2,700 / 2,900 and 2,800 / 3,000 ETB pairs. | Confirm the apparent grade/rate ordering. |
| Article 13.7 minimum, pp. 7–8 | Applies 19.2 MT to an initial ordinary grade run and each selected rerun. Add-ons use actual clean MT. | Confirm the scope of the floor for special routes, reruns, add-ons, and combined lots. |
| Articles 14–18 storage dates, pp. 8–10 | Uses entered whole days; illustrates “three consecutive months” as 90 days and one fixed bag count per period. | Choose calendar rules, chargeable receipt/loading days, raw-to-output cutoff, and partial-stock policy. |

The page is a client walkthrough and draft estimator. It stores no client records, performs no approvals, and cannot release stock. Any live ERP needs dated, authorized documents, a stock ledger, payment reconciliation, and a separate reviewed invoice-posting step.
