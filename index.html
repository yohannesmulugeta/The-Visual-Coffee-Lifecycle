    Core Operational Documents & Data Model

    Proposed operational records

    These four record groups are a proposed ERP design. The contract requires signed receipts, requests and release evidence, but does not prescribe this database schema or QR tags.

    Voucher #1 • Articles 5, 6, 10

    Coffee & Bag GRN (የገቢ ደረሰኝ)

    Starts Raw Clock

    Required Fields: Client Name, Legal Agent Name & POA Ref, ECTA Waybill No. (ሸኚ ደብዳቤ), Truck Plate & Seal No., Coffee Type (Washed/Unwashed) & Origin, Bag Count (በኬሻ), Gross/Tare/Net Weight (kg), Warehouse & Stack Tag ID (ታግ).

    Proposed system action: Create a client lot and receipt record; record unloading only if performed. A QR tag is optional.

    Voucher #2 • Articles 7, 12, 23.2

    Silo Job Card & Generator Consent (የዝግጅት ትዕዛዝ)

    Stops Raw Clock

    Required Fields: FIFO Queue No., Target Export Grade (G1–G5), Screen Size (14 vs 15+), Packaging Spec (Single, Double, GrainPro, Carton, Vacuum), Bag Printing Design Approval, Generator Diesel Consent Signature.

    Proposed system action: Record stock moved to processing, packaging consumed and actual silo labor. The raw storage cutoff needs client confirmation.

    Voucher #3 • Articles 17.1, 18.1, 20

    Production Output Receipt (የምርት መረከቢያ ሰነድ)

    Starts Output Clocks

    Required Fields: Clean Coffee Output (MT & Bags), Reject/By-Product Output (MT & Bags), Leftover Raw Coffee, Parchment Retained (20% Washed), Dust Shrinkage (≤2.5%).

    Proposed system action: Reconcile outputs and start the clean and reject storage clocks from their receipts; review the minimum-charge scope.

    Voucher #4 • Articles 3.3, 4.4–4.6, 22.2

    Final Dispatch & Gate Pass (የወጪ ደረሰኝ)

    Requires Paid Invoice

    Required Fields: Client Official Loading Letter Ref, ECTA Reject Transfer Permit (if ECX), Finance Clearance Stamp (100% Paid), Dispatched Clean Bags/Container No., Returned Empty Bags Count.

    Proposed system action: Check payment, release documents and stock before issuing a signed goods issue. Partial dispatch must leave the remaining balance open.

    Pre-Development Sign-Off Checklist

    ERP Engineering Assumptions & Contract Clarifications

    Review these 6 architectural assumptions with Hayked General Trading management before finalizing the database schema.

    Contract Anomaly #1 Article 13.1 Table

    Unclear Row 13.1.13 & Unwashed Grade 4/5 Prices

    The scanned contract (Page 6) shows a short but unclear Amharic label for 13.1.13 and prices of 3,000 / 3,200 ETB. It also prints 13.1.6 Grade 4 at 2,700 / 2,900 and 13.1.7 Grade 5 at 2,800 / 3,000. Keep those printed values until Hayked confirms both points.

    Assumption #2 Articles 14, 15, 17

    How should three months be counted?

    The contract says "three consecutive months." This demo uses 90 days only as an illustration. Hayked must choose calendar-month or fixed-day boundaries and confirm the chargeable start/end days.

    Assumption #3 Articles 15.1 & 17.1

    Exact Cut-Off Date Between Raw & Processed Storage

    Output receipt dates start the processed and reject clocks. The exact raw-stock cutoff and treatment of the processing day need agreement, especially for partially processed lots.

    Assumption #4 Article 15.4

    Retroactive Re-Billing on Ownership Transfer

    Article 15.4 changes storage treatment when raw coffee transfers to another client. The first owner's retrospective charge and the second owner's later exit need a documented billing decision before automation.

    Assumption #5 Articles 20.1 & 20.3

    20% Washed Parchment Retention & Mass Balance

    Record washed parchment separately from dust/loss and distinguish its ownership. The draft estimate checks the stated 20% parchment and 2.5% dust limits; a real stock adjustment still needs review.

    Assumption #6 Articles 3.3, 4.4 & 22

    Payment clearance and debt escalation

    Payment and release documents must be checked before handover. This static demo cannot enforce a gate. Articles 15.5 and 22 describe warning and lawful debt recovery; valuation and escalation need management review.
